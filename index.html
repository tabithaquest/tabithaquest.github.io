Nonprofit Capital for
Affordable Medicines

Tabitha Quest was founded to make life-saving medicines accessible and affordable for patients and our healthcare system. We believe accessibility should be a core objective of drug development and integrated across development, manufacturing, and pricing.

Our nonprofit-controlled structure combines philanthropic and other mission-driven capital with private investment to support drug development. Affordability shapes decisions from how medicines are financed and developed to how they are manufactured and ultimately priced. This structure allows an increasing share of the economics to be directed toward lower prices, broader patient access, domestic manufacturing, and the development of additional medicines.
